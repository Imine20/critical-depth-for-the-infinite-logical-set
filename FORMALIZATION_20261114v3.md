## Proof obligations

- For the geometric step in Theorem 2(2), take the real coordinate ring modulo the ideal vanishing on the actual real gate solutions. Minimal primes and separation of evaluations give a component with a transcendental projective coordinate. Clear denominators of a derivation to obtain a polynomial vector field preserving the component ideal. A local ODE solution preserves the finitely many generators by uniqueness for a homogeneous linear ODE and has nonzero derivative of the chosen coordinate. In the unitary cover, a nonscalar skew-Hermitian tangent, its adjoint conjugates, and the scalar tangent span the skew-Hermitians. Apply the inverse function theorem in a Cayley chart to a finite product of curves, then descend to the projective group. The manuscript uses semialgebraic stratification for curve extraction.
- For the quantum exponent, a finite fault ensemble assigns each history a positive constant times its Bernoulli power. A common recovery for all histories up to order (m-1) supplies the upper loss bound. If no common recovery exists for histories up to order (m), compactness of the finite-dimensional CPTP set and continuity of their total conditional infidelity yield a strictly positive uniform lower bound even for noise-dependent recovery. The KL bridge must use arbitrary propagated unitary errors.
- In Q37, the phase certificates can be expressed as degree at most three Boolean multilinear polynomials modulo eight for the logical (T) layer, and degree at most two modulo four for the square-root layer. The distance certificate can search for collisions of syndrome and different logical labels among masks of weight at most three. This is equivalent to the absence of a nontrivial logical mask of weight at most six. See [Dua et al., Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06730v1#A2).
