## Scope of the source theorem

The physical space is a tensor product of \(n\ge1\) sites with arbitrary finite local dimensions. The formal model uses finite complex matrices indexed by the product of local bases. It does not export a separate transport API from arbitrary Lean Hilbert-space carrier structures. `Manuscript.Code` records an isometric encoding

\[
V:\mathbb C^{\ell}\otimes\mathbb C^g\longrightarrow\mathcal H,
\qquad V^\dagger V=I,
\]

with \(\ell\ge2\), \(g\ge1\), and \(d\ge2\). `Manuscript.DetectsBelow C d` requires every operator supported on fewer than \(d\) sites to compress as \(I_\ell\otimes M\). This includes subsystem codes and permits arbitrary gauge action. A realized logical unitary satisfies exactly \(UV=V(W\otimes Z)\), with \(W,Z\) unitary; it need not act trivially on the gauge subsystem.

Each layer is an arbitrary finite list of unitary gates on pairwise disjoint supports, each of cardinality at most \(q\). The gate matrices are unrestricted. The current manuscript uses a **common total-depth budget** \(h\ge1\): its image contains every such circuit of depth \(0\le r\le h\). For \(q\ge2\), Theorem 1 asserts a finite projective image when

\[
h<2\lceil\log_q d\rceil-1.
\]

Non-density is in the actual quotient \(PU(\ell)\) with its quotient topology. The claim concerns the bounded-budget image; it does not assert finiteness of the group generated by arbitrarily long products of its elements.

The accepted core development is stronger: it also allows the budget \(h=0\) and proves the unary case \(q=1\) at every finite budget. These are library extensions outside the current paper's Theorem 1, not additional cases asserted in that statement. Depth-zero circuits are still included in an at-most image with a positive budget.

## Circuit and budget correspondence

`CircuitNormalization.lean` defines `ArbitraryLayer` and `ArbitraryCircuit` using finite gate lists. Empty-support gates contribute unit-modulus scalars. Removing them preserves the logical action after adjusting the gauge unitary. Pairwise disjoint nonempty supports leave at most \(n\) gates per layer. Their order is retained and identity gates on empty supports pad the list to the `Fin n` slot model. No physical sites or ancillary factors are added. `arbitraryLogicalImage_eq_logicalImage` states equality of the two exact-depth logical images.

`BudgetSemantics.lean` defines `arbitraryLogicalImageAtMost` as the union over depths \(0,\ldots,h\). Its finiteness follows from exact-depth finiteness at those finitely many depths. This uses neither a generated-group closure nor an assumption that exact-depth images are monotone. The accepted core target is `Manuscript.theoremOneAtMost`; its closing declaration, `Manuscript.theoremOneAtMost_proved` in `MainResults.lean`, specializes to the current paper by retaining the \(q\ge2\) branch and assuming \(h\ge1\).

`PositiveDepth.lean` restricts the stronger theorem to the current \(h\ge1,q\ge2\) statement. It also handles the current manuscript's minimum over positive budgets: `hInfinityPositiveAtMost_eq_hInfinityAtMost` identifies it with the nonnegative minimum whenever budget zero has a finite image. Theorem 2 derives that finiteness from the actual code and distance assumptions. The bridge and its examples passed the current replay and audit.

## Alternate algebraic proof

The Lean development takes an algebraic route to finiteness. It does **not** formalize the manuscript's semialgebraic/CAD decomposition into finitely many smooth pieces. The common local ingredients are two-sided light cones, distance detection, and rigidity of the logical action. The final passage to a finite image is replaced as follows.

1. **Present each support arrangement algebraically.** `CircuitParameters.lean` defines a finite-variable complex polynomial algebra and its quotient `CircuitCoordinateRing`. Variables describe local matrices and inverse-coordinate matrices, together with \(W,W^{-1},Z,Z^{-1}\). Relations impose two-sided inverse identities for the physically embedded gates and the logical/gauge matrices, plus
   \[
   UV=V(W\otimes Z),\qquad V^\dagger U=(W\otimes Z)V^\dagger.
   \]
   Local inverse identities before physical embedding are not imposed. This is a general-linear algebraic presentation containing all actual unitary implementations. `Manuscript.actualCircuitPoint` in `CircuitPoints.lean` constructs their quotient-algebra points. Its inverse coordinates evaluate at adjoints; `point_encoded_dual` derives the second encoded relation from the original unitary implementation equation.

2. **Prove derivative vanishing for every field-valued point.** `Manuscript.solutionFieldLayer` in `CircuitSolutions.lean` extracts genuine supported gate/inverse pairs from any algebra homomorphism to a field extension of \(\mathbb C\). Scalar extension transports the original detection property to their supported linear spans. The actual product rule expresses \(D(U)U^{-1}\) as gate insertions. At a split with `before + 1 + after = h`, the two supported representatives have bounds \(q^{\mathrm{after}+1}\) and \(q^{\mathrm{before}+1}\). One is at most \(q^{\lceil h/2\rceil}\). Under the strict distance bound, detection and the encoded change of frame make each contribution gauge-only. `Manuscript.fieldLayerSequence_derivative_mem_gaugeOnly` in `InsertionDetection.lean` supplies this conclusion. `encoded_projective_coordinates_derivation_zero` in `AlgebraicRigidity.lean` then proves
   \[
   D\bigl(W_{ab}(W^{-1})_{ce}\bigr)=0
   \]
   for every base-linear derivation. `Manuscript.circuitPoint_projective_derivation_zero` discharges this premise from the code, layout, and depth hypotheses.

3. **Turn derivative constants into finite evaluation images.** `isAlgebraic_of_forall_derivation_eq_zero` in `FieldConstants.lean` proves the characteristic-zero field criterion using a transcendence basis, a polynomial partial derivation, and extension through localization and a separable algebraic extension. `DerivationExtension.lean` constructs the extension through the Kähler-differential equivalence for formally étale algebras. No finite-generation assumption on the resulting fraction field is substituted for this argument. Over the algebraically closed base \(\mathbb C\), `ScalarConstants.lean` identifies these constants with base scalars. The coordinate algebra is Noetherian, so it has finitely many minimal primes. `isIntegral_of_scalar_mod_minimalPrimes` in `ComponentConstants.lean` combines the component scalars into a monic polynomial whose value is nilpotent, then takes a power to obtain an annihilating polynomial. It does not require the coordinate algebra to be reduced. `evaluation_image_finite_of_all_field_derivations` in `AlgebraicFiniteImage.lean`, using `integral_coordinate_evaluation_image_finite` in `IntegralImage.lean`, gives finitely many evaluation tuples.

4. **Recover projective logical finiteness.** At actual unitary points, these tuples are the entries of conjugation by \(W\), namely \(W_{ab}(W^\dagger)_{ce}\). `unitary_conjugationCoordinates_eq_iff_projectivelyEquivalent` in `ProjectiveFaithfulness.lean` proves that equal conjugation coordinates mean equality modulo one global unit phase. `Manuscript.supportLogicalMatrices_projective_finite` obtains finiteness for each support arrangement. `logicalImage_finite_of_cone` takes a finite union over support functions, while gate matrices remain arbitrary. `Arithmetic.below_threshold_iff` supplies the paper's threshold; `Arithmetic.unary_cone_bound` supplies the separate library extension \(q=1\). The exact-depth core closing declaration is `Manuscript.theoremOne_proved`.

`ProjectiveTopology.lean` defines `ProjectiveUnitary` from the subtype of actual unitary matrices. Closed conjugation-coordinate fibers give its T1 property; explicit distinct unitary phase classes show it is infinite for \(\ell\ge2\). A finite image is therefore closed and non-dense there. `Manuscript.theoremOneAtMost_of_theoremOne` in `BudgetSemantics.lean` combines this conclusion with finite-list normalization and the common depth budget.
