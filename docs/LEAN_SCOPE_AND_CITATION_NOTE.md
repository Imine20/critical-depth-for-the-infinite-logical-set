# Lean scope and Bonet-Monroig citation

## Formal result scope

The Lean development proves the statements corresponding to the two main theorems under their stated circuit and code hypotheses. Theorem 1 covers finite-dimensional subsystem codes with all-operator detection below d and one common total-depth budget; its conclusion is finiteness and non-density of the exact projective logical image. The formal proof of Theorem 1 uses an independent algebraic argument. It does not formalize the End Matter CAD/smooth-cell construction line by line. The manuscript's literature discussion, fault-tolerance commentary and arbitrary products of shallow gates are not additional formal theorem claims.
